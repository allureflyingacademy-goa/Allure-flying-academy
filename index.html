ABOUT ALLURE

Meet the Founder

Aafreen Shaikh Lad

Founder & Director, Allure Flying Academy

“The right guidance can turn ambition into a successful career.”

Allure Flying Academy was founded with a simple belief — the right guidance can turn ambition into a successful career.

With more than 10 years of professional experience in customer service, I have learned that exceptional service is about much more than simply doing a job. It is about communication, confidence, empathy, presentation, professionalism, and the ability to make people feel valued.

This experience became the inspiration behind Allure Flying Academy — an academy created to help aspiring professionals build the skills, confidence, and personality they need to step into the world of aviation, hospitality, and customer service.

At Allure, we don't focus only on what you need to learn. We focus on who you need to become — confident, well-groomed, articulate, professional, and ready to face the industry with confidence.

Every student who walks through our doors brings a unique dream and a different story. I believe in recognizing that individuality, nurturing their strengths, and helping them discover their potential.

Your dream career starts with one decision — the decision to begin. Join Allure Flying Academy and take the first step towards becoming the professional you aspire to be.
